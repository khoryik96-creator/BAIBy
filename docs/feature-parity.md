# v5.3 feature parity

The current Unity nursery covers early infancy with illustrated newborn, sitter, and crawler stages. The archived HTML spans infancy through adulthood and has a wider set of stats, activities, events, and outcomes. This inventory compares the implementations in the repository. [Archived prototype](../legacy/BAIby_v5.3.html) · [Unity nursery](../unity/Assets/BAIby/Presentation/NurseryPreview.cs)

| Area | HTML v5.3 | Current Unity nursery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adoption and settings | Name, boy/girl choice, Normal or Developer mode. The nursery has theme and sound toggles, customization, and a confirmed reset. | Name and adoption. No gender choice, theme or sound controls, or player-facing reset. Preview day advance is available in the nursery. |
| Needs and stats | Main screen shows Hunger, Happiness, Health, Intelligence, Social, and Fitness. Hunger rises after feeding, so its number functions as fullness. Creativity and kindness are tracked. Trauma, abuse count, injuries, junk score, and weight delta also affect reactions, traits, or reports. | Fullness, Comfort, Energy, Cleanliness, and Health. Comfort covers part of the original mood behavior. Health improves when basic needs stay met and declines when a need stays very low. The Development panel tracks story sessions and connection. Intelligence, Fitness, and the other historical counters have no direct equivalents yet. |
| Daily care and activities | 38 choices across Feed, Play, Learn, Care, and Discipline. There are 12 foods, six play choices, six learning choices, six care choices, and eight discipline choices. Each category allows two actions per day. Cards use mouse drag or touch hold-and-drag. | Milk, mashed banana, soft oats, Soothe, Rest, Change diaper, Read a story, and Play with either a rattle or a plush. The crawler stage unlocks both soft foods. Two toys retain separate familiarity and can become a favorite. Buttons support tap. Day-based category limits and dedicated Learn and Discipline categories remain open. |
| Growth, time, and neglect | Newborn → Toddler → Child → Teen → Adult. The first feed per day counts toward stage advancement. Stage targets are 7, 7, 7, and 14 fed days. Normal mode uses a local-date gate on day advance. Three consecutive missed feeding days end the run. | Newborn → Early sitter → Crawler with distinct poses and abilities. Age advances with elapsed time, and absence has capped need decay. No later childhood or adult stages and no missed-feeding game over. |
| Traits and reporting | Feeding history, streak, day and stage progress, status alerts, trait labels, personality archetypes, a Baby Report, and long-term outlook. Adult completion gives a summary. Accumulated harmful responses can trigger a separate game over. | Age, stage, needs, favorite toy, and a Development panel with story sessions and connection. No feeding history, broader traits, long-term outlook, or endings. |
| Incidents and diaper care | 14 choice-driven incidents across newborn through teen stages. Responses change stats and can contribute to the harmful-response counter. A newborn/toddler diaper accident opens a swipe-to-clean Canvas interaction. | Change diaper offers either a short tap or swipe cleanup activity or a quick change. Both restore Cleanliness and show a fresh diaper cue. A new sound unlocks at the sitter stage with two different care responses. The broader incident catalog remains open. |
| Character expression and customization | Canvas rendering, action and tap reactions, stat and trait-dependent dialogue, synthesized sound, and skin, hair, outfit, accessory, hat, and mood options. | Illustrated awake and resting stage variants, newborn tracking, sitter reaching, and crawler movement toward a familiar toy. The new sound event has a chime cue. Cosmetic choices and broader nursery audio remain open. |
| Saves | Browser `localStorage` holds the active baby and tab, with separate theme and sound preferences. | Versioned Unity save v7 supports earlier Unity schemas. No import of the historical HTML save. |

The capability list is grounded in the original action catalogs, state and trait rules, growth and report functions, incident and minigame code, and customization options. Unity entries follow the current simulation state and visible interaction controls. ## Priorities for the next slices

1. **Make care choices consequential.** Build on Health and diaper care with a small age-appropriate catalog. Build on the feeding menu by adding distinguishable effects for activities such as bathing. Keep Fullness, Comfort, and Energy visible and add readable behavior when a need is low. Give activity definitions stable IDs and test multi-stat outcomes and save migration.
2. **Restore development feedback.** Add a simple report showing milestones, care history, and interests. Build on story sessions and connection by adding Learning and Social activities that suit the baby's stage. Add motor or Fitness progress as movement expands, and expose only outcomes the baby can express in play.
3. **Add choices and variety.** Build on A new sound with more age-appropriate events. Extend the interactive diaper care when more nursery activities are ready. Then add cosmetics, sound, and confirmed restart. Expand into toddler and later life stages after the infant loop supports varied play and clear development.
4. **Decide which older rules to carry forward.** Keep elapsed-time growth and capped offline need decay. Review strict daily action quotas, punitive endings, harmful discipline, body/personality labels, and browser-save import as product decisions. The historical Baby Report can inform a growth journal that reflects observed interests and milestones.

Newborn, sitter, and crawler are subdivisions of infancy in the restart. Elapsed-time growth is an intentional change from feeding-based ages. The other gaps in the table remain open unless the design explicitly changes them. [Restart roadmap](roadmap.md)
