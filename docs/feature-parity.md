# v5.3 feature parity

The current Unity nursery covers infancy through the first steps, with illustrated chibi newborn, sitter, crawler, and early walker stages. The archived HTML spans infancy through adulthood and has a wider set of stats, activities, events, and outcomes. This inventory compares the implementations in the repository. [Archived prototype](../legacy/BAIby_v5.3.html) · [Unity nursery](../unity/Assets/BAIby/Presentation/NurseryPreview.cs)

| Area | HTML v5.3 | Current Unity nursery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adoption and settings | Name, boy/girl choice, Normal or Developer mode. The nursery has theme and sound toggles, customization, and a confirmed reset. | Name, adoption, and a saved Sage or Peach room style. Gender choice, a sound toggle, and a player-facing reset remain open. Preview day advance is available in the nursery. The capability list is grounded in the original action catalogs, state and trait rules, growth and report functions, incident and minigame code, and customization options. Unity entries follow the current simulation state and visible interaction controls. ## Priorities for the next slices

1. **Make care choices consequential.** Build on Health and diaper care with a small age-appropriate catalog. Expand the feeding and hygiene menus with activities that have distinguishable effects. Keep Fullness, Comfort, and Energy visible and add readable behavior when a need is low. Give activity definitions stable IDs and test multi-stat outcomes and save migration.
2. **Restore development feedback.** Build on the Baby Report by adding more care history and Learning and Social activities that suit the baby's stage. Add motor or Fitness progress as movement expands, and expose only outcomes the baby can express in play.
3. **Add choices and variety.** Build on A new sound with more age-appropriate events. Extend the interactive diaper care when more nursery activities are ready. Baby cosmetics are in place; add sound and confirmed restart next. Expand into toddler and later life stages after the infant loop supports varied play and clear development.
4. **Decide which older rules to carry forward.** Keep elapsed-time growth and capped offline need decay. Review strict daily action quotas, punitive endings, harmful discipline, body/personality labels, and browser-save import as product decisions. The historical Baby Report can inform a growth journal that reflects observed interests and milestones.

The early walker marks the transition out of infancy in the restart. Elapsed-time growth is an intentional change from feeding-based ages. The other gaps in the table remain open unless the design explicitly changes them. [Restart roadmap](roadmap.md)
